# Procedure: charging a LiPo pack

- **Applies to:** Battery, PWR-001, decision 001
- **Time:** 60 min
- **Last updated:** 2026-11-26

## You need
- Balance charger set to LiPo 3S
- LiPo-safe charging bag

## Steps
1. Check the pack for swelling or damage; do not charge a swollen pack
2. Put the pack in the LiPo-safe bag on a non-flammable surface
3. Connect both the main lead and the balance lead to the charger
4. Select LiPo, 3S, balance charge, 1C current
5. Stay nearby until the charger reports full (4.2 V per cell)
6. Disconnect, then label the pack as charged with the date

> **Warning:** Never charge without the balance lead: cells drift apart and can be overcharged

> **Warning:** Never leave a charging pack unattended
